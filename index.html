Biography

Freedomination is a metal band from Vantaa, Finland, combining thrash, progressive, heavy metal, and punk rock influences into a powerful, contemporary sound. Gaining recognition in the Finnish metal scene, the band was featured in Inferno magazine and received acclaim for its self-published releases.

After a hiatus since 2017, they returned in 2022 with a renewed lineup and have now released their debut album, Until the Lights Go Out.
